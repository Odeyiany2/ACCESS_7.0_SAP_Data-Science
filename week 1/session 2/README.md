## Assignment: Python Foundations in Practice

**Scenario: A small retail shop**

A shop wants to understand its sales and identify which products need restocking.

Use the following information:

| Product | Unit price (₦) | Quantity sold | Stock remaining |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Notebook | 1,200 | 18 | 8 |
| Pen | 350 | 40 | 25 |
| Calculator | 5,500 | 6 | 4 |
| School bag | 8,000 | 5 | 12 |
| Water bottle | 2,000 | 10 | 7 |

### Your tasks

**Part A – Variables and calculations**

1. Create variables for the name, unit price, quantity sold and remaining stock of at least three products.
2. Calculate the revenue for each product (unit price × quantity sold).
3. Print each product's name and revenue.

**Part B – Conditional statements**

4. The shop wants to identify products with stock of 10 units or fewer. Use a conditional statement to print `"Restock"` for those products and `"Stock is sufficient"` for the others.
5. Check whether the revenue from each product is greater than ₦20,000.

**Part C – Loops**

6. Store the product names in a list and use a loop to print each name.

**Part D – Functions**

7. Write a function called `calculate_revenue` that accepts a unit price and quantity sold, then returns the revenue and test it.

### Submission requirements

- Complete your work in a Google Colab notebook.
- Include short Markdown explanations for what your code does.
- Make sure your code cells run without errors.
- Save your notebook with a clear name, such as `Session_2_Python_Assignment.ipynb`.
- Upload your assignment to your GitHub repository.
- Submit your notebook link in the [assignment submission spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d5WZvTn8jjHXPDTw3V4Bdxmo0Aqwuoem8JZp_EpCDVg/edit?usp=sharing) under your own sheet name.

**Note:** You do not need to use NumPy or Pandas for this assignment. We'll introduce those in the next session.
